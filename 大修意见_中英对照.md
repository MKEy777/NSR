# 大修意见汇总（中英对照）

> **论文**: "An Asynchronous Neuromorphic Architecture for Wearable EEG Emotion Recognition"
> **期刊**: National Science Review (NSR)
> **审稿人**: 4 位 | **意见总数**: 42 条

## Reviewer 1

**Overall Comment**

> This manuscript proposes a jointly optimized asynchronous neuromorphic architecture for wearable EEG emotion recognition, integrating a Dynamic Adaptive Spiking Neural Network (DA-SNN) with a globally asynchronous locally synchronous (GALS) hardware accelerator. The authors evaluate the proposed framework on multiple datasets, and the results demonstrate its effectiveness and potential for deployment. The cross-layer design that combines algorithmic sparsity with hardware-level optimization is interesting, and the work shows certain novelty with potential practical relevance in wearable scenarios. However, several issues need to be addressed:

**中文大意**: 本文提出 DA-SNN + GALS 加速器的跨层联合设计，在多个数据集上验证有效，工作有新颖性和实用潜力，但存在若干问题需解决。

---

### R1-Q1

> Although results are reported on five datasets, it is not clear how the experiments are actually conducted. For instance, the 96.98% accuracy on SEED is reported without specifying whether it is based on subject-dependent training or a cross-session setting. The same issue appears for DEAP and DREAMER, where the data splitting strategy is not described.

**中文大意**: 五个数据集的实验方式不透明——SEED 的 96.98% 未说明是 subject-dependent 还是 cross-session；DEAP 和 DREAMER 的数据划分策略也未交代。

---

### R1-Q2

> The role of some key components is also not very clear. The paper attributes performance gains to the adaptive temporal window and the gating mechanisms, but the main text does not really show what is happening in practice. The supplementary material includes some theoretical discussion, but in the main paper there is little evidence to support these claims.

**中文大意**: 关键组件（adaptive temporal window、gating mechanisms）的实际作用缺乏证据支撑。正文几乎没有展示它们在实践中如何起效，补充材料虽有理论讨论但正文证据不足。

---

### R1-Q3

> The ablation study is quite limited. It is only done on the SEED dataset, and the main modules are not tested on DEAP or DREAMER, where the signal properties and channel configurations are different. Without these results, it is difficult to tell whether the improvements are consistent across datasets.

**中文大意**: 消融实验仅在 SEED 上做，DEAP 和 DREAMER（信号特性与通道配置不同）均未测试，无法判断各模块提升是否跨数据集一致。

---

### R1-Q4

> The hardware section is not very easy to follow. In Fig. 4(a), the interaction between the synchronous and asynchronous parts is not clearly illustrated, and the handshake mechanism is only briefly mentioned. It is also not very clear how the claimed energy efficiency of the GALS design is achieved, for example, how it relates to event sparsity or clock gating.

**中文大意**: 硬件部分难以理解——Fig. 4(a) 中同步/异步部分的交互不清，handshake mechanism 仅简要提及；GALS 设计如何借助 event sparsity 或 clock gating 实现能效优势也未说清楚。

---

### R1-Q5

> There are also some inconsistencies in terminology. Terms like "temporal window," "time window," and "spiking window" are used in different places without clear distinctions. The model naming is also not consistent (e.g., DA-SNN, ATSNN, adaptive SNN). In Table 1, metrics such as Acc, F1, and Spe are reported, but their definitions are not clearly given in the main text.

**中文大意**: 术语使用不一致——"temporal window""time window""spiking window"混用未区分；模型命名不统一（DA-SNN / ATSNN / adaptive SNN）；Table 1 中 Acc、F1、Spe 等指标在正文中未给出明确定义。

---

### R1-Q6

> Fig. 4 does not clearly indicate whether the data is represented as tensors or spike events, which makes the processing pipeline difficult to follow.

**中文大意**: Fig. 4 未标明数据是以 tensor 还是 spike events 形式表示，导致整个处理流水线难以理解。

---

### R1-Q7

> The introduction provides general background, but the specific research gap is not stated very clearly. The motivation for combining asynchronous hardware with EEG-based SNNs could be explained more directly, especially in relation to existing methods.

**中文大意**: 引言给出了泛泛的背景，但具体 research gap 不够清晰；异步硬件与 EEG-based SNN 结合的动机应更直接地阐述，尤其是相对于现有方法。

## Reviewer 2

**Overall Comment**

> This paper proposes an end-to-end asynchronous neuromorphic architecture for wearable EEG emotion recognition. The authors achieve joint optimization from algorithms to hardware: at the algorithm level, they introduce a lightweight Dynamic Adaptive Spiking Neural Network (DA-SNN) that utilizes a Depthwise Separable Gating Module (DSGM) for feature extraction and a Division-Free Time-to-First-Spike (DF-TTFS) encoding to convert dense EEG signals into sparse spike events. At the hardware level, a Globally Asynchronous Locally Synchronous (GALS) accelerator is designed to perform event-driven computation. The system achieves a high accuracy of 96.98% on the SEED dataset. Hardware evaluation demonstrates a low energy consumption of 2.66 µJ per inference and a dynamic power of 40 mW. Overall, this work provides a highly promising, energy-efficient solution for resource-constrained wearable emotion monitoring.
>
> However, there are several critical issues that must be addressed before the manuscript can be considered for publication:

**中文大意**: 本文实现了从算法（DSGM + DF-TTFS + DA-SNN）到硬件（GALS accelerator）的端到端联合优化，SEED 达 96.98%，推理功耗 2.66 µJ / 40 mW，方向很有前景。但以下关键问题必须在发表前解决：

---

### R2-Q1

> In the efficiency analysis, the hardware comparison is fundamentally unbalanced. The energy efficiency of the proposed GALS accelerator, deployed on a customized FPGA, is compared directly against conventional lightweight CNNs running on a general-purpose ARM processor (Raspberry Pi 5). This "apples-to-oranges" comparison fails to objectively demonstrate the true architectural advantages of the asynchronous design against appropriate hardware-level baselines.

**中文大意**: 硬件对比严重不公平——定制 FPGA 上的 GALS 加速器 vs 通用 ARM 处理器（Raspberry Pi 5）上的轻量 CNN，是典型的 "apples-to-oranges" 比较，无法客观反映异步架构的真正优势。应与适当的硬件级 baseline 对比。

---

### R2-Q2

> Affective EEG signals are highly subject-dependent. While the paper reports exceptionally high accuracies across datasets, it fails to explicitly state whether these results are derived from "within-subject" or "cross-subject" (leave-one-subject-out) cross-validation. Given the wearable context, the omission of clear cross-subject generalization metrics is a major empirical flaw.

**中文大意**: 情感 EEG 高度 subject-dependent，但文中未明确是 within-subject 还是 cross-subject (leave-one-subject-out) 验证。考虑到可穿戴场景，缺少 cross-subject 泛化指标是重大实证缺陷。

---

### R2-Q3

> The methodology relies heavily on Linear Dynamical System (LDS) smoothing to filter the feature sequences across windows. LDS smoothing inherently requires observing sequences over time, which introduces significant computational latency, memory buffering, and complex matrix operations. The manuscript entirely fails to address how this computationally expensive, latency-inducing smoothing process is executed in real-time on the proposed resource-constrained edge hardware. Ignoring the front-end preprocessing cost renders the "real-time wearable" deployment claims highly questionable.

**中文大意**: LDS 平滑需要跨时间窗观察序列，引入了显著的 computational latency、memory buffering 和复杂矩阵运算，但文中完全未讨论这一前端预处理如何在资源受限的边缘硬件上实时执行。忽略预处理开销使 "real-time wearable" 的声称非常可疑。

---

### R2-Q4

> The authors acknowledge in the Discussion that long-term wearable deployment faces severe challenges from individual state variations and electrode impedance drift. However, for a system whose primary selling point is enabling "continuous wearable monitoring," the complete absence of any implemented online adaptive or calibration mechanism is a critical functional gap in the proposed framework.

**中文大意**: 讨论部分承认长期可穿戴部署面临个体状态变化和 electrode impedance drift 的挑战，但系统的核心卖点是 "continuous wearable monitoring"，却完全没有任何 online adaptive 或 calibration mechanism，这是关键的功能缺口。

---

### R2-Q5

> The Globally Asynchronous Locally Synchronous (GALS) architecture relies on a complex four-phase handshake protocol and Address-Event Representation (AER) routing to cross clock domains. The authors praise the energy reduction from sparse compute activation but completely ignore the significant dynamic power, physical area footprint, and latency overhead inherently introduced by the AER routing logic, threshold filters, and metastability-prevention synchronizers. Without a transparent, component-level breakdown of the GALS interface overhead, the isolated 40 mW dynamic power figure is incomplete and highly misleading.

**中文大意**: GALS 架构依赖复杂的四相 handshake 协议和 AER 路由来跨时钟域，但文中只强调稀疏计算的能耗降低，完全忽略了 AER routing logic、threshold filters、metastability-prevention synchronizers 引入的动态功耗、面积和延迟开销。没有组件级的 GALS 接口开销分解，40 mW 的动态功耗数据是不完整且高度误导的。

---

### R2-Q6

> There is a highly noticeable and strange inconsistency in the manuscript's formatting. Specifically, Table 1 and Table 2 are completely different in size, scale, and layout. This glaring visual discrepancy looks highly unprofessional and severely disrupts the reading experience.

**中文大意**: 格式明显不一致——Table 1 和 Table 2 在大大小、比例、布局上完全不同，视觉上非常不专业，严重影响阅读体验。

---

## Reviewer 3

**Overall Comment**

> This work investigates an asynchronous neuromorphic solution for wearable EEG-based emotion recognition, where a spiking neural model is coupled with a GALS-based hardware design. The study attempts to leverage temporal sparsity at the algorithmic level while aligning it with efficient hardware execution. Experimental results on several public datasets indicate that the approach is viable and achieves competitive performance. Despite these strengths, there remain several aspects that would benefit from further clarification and improvement:

**中文大意**: 本文将 SNN 与 GALS 硬件设计结合，利用算法级 temporal sparsity 对接高效硬件执行，在多个公开数据集上表现可行且有竞争力。但仍有多处需要进一步澄清和改进：

### R3-Q1

> The manuscript reports only average performance metrics across datasets, without providing measures of variability such as standard deviation. Considering the inherent variability in EEG signals across subjects and trials, including statistical dispersion would improve the credibility and robustness of the reported results.

**中文大意**: 只报告了平均值，缺少 standard deviation 等变异性指标。考虑到 EEG 信号固有的跨被试和跨试次变异，加入统计离散度能提升结果的可信度。

In addition, the model naming (e.g., DA-SNN, ATSNN, adaptive SNN) is not consistent throughout the paper, which may lead to confusion. A more consistent use of terminology would improve the overall readability.

**中文大意**: "temporal window""time window""spiking window" 混用且未区分含义；模型命名（DA-SNN / ATSNN / adaptive SNN）不一致，易造成混淆，统一术语可提高可读性。（与 R1-Q5 一致）

**中文大意**: 噪声、量化等条件下的评估缺少同一设置下的 reference baseline，加入基线曲线能使鲁棒性声称更具说服力。

### R3-Q4

**中文大意**: EEG 特征 → 编码 → SNN 之间各阶段的数据变换描述不够详细，需要更清晰地说明端到端流水线。

**中文大意**: 训练 epochs、优化策略、硬件配置等重要实验设置仅简要提及或分散在各章节，整合这些细节有助于提高可复现性。

### R3-Q6

**中文大意**: 强调可穿戴应用潜力，但对 latency stability、memory footprint、实际部署场景等实用约束讨论不足，扩展此部分能增强工作的实际意义。

## Reviewer 4

**Overall Comment**

> This manuscript proposes an asynchronous neuromorphic architecture for wearable EEG emotion recognition, combining a Dynamic Adaptive Spiking Neural Network (DA-SNN), a depthwise separable gating module (DSGM), a division-free TTFS encoder, an adaptive temporal spiking neural network (ATSNN), and a GALS FPGA accelerator. The work is potentially valuable because it attempts a cross-layer co-design between EEG feature extraction, spike-time coding, and low-power event-driven hardware. However, several mathematical, methodological, notation, validation, and presentation issues must be addressed before the paper can meet the standard required for publication in *National Science Review*. In particular, the original B1 model paper by Stanojevic et al. should be discussed in much greater depth, since the ATSNN formulation, TTFS coding, and claimed gradient-stability motivation appear to rely substantially on that theoretical framework. The manuscript should more clearly distinguish what is inherited from B1, what is modified, and what is genuinely new in the proposed DA-SNN/ATSNN architecture. Therefore, while the paper has value, publication would require major revision. I invite the authors to address the following observations.

**中文大意**: 本文尝试跨层协同设计（EEG 特征提取 + spike-time coding + 低功耗 event-driven 硬件），有潜在价值。但存在数学、方法、符号、验证和表述等多方面问题。尤其关键的是：ATSNN 的 formulation、TTFS coding 和梯度稳定性声称严重依赖 Stanojevic 等人的 B1 model 理论框架，但讨论深度不足。需明确区分哪些继承自 B1、哪些做了修改、哪些是真正新增的。

---

### R4-Q1 — EEG sparsity vs model-induced sparsity

> The current wording suggests that affective EEG is intrinsically event-driven and temporally sparse, and that the hardware directly exploits this raw EEG sparsity. However, the model first transforms EEG into windowed PSE feature maps, applies spatial mapping and LDS smoothing, and only then imposes spike-time sparsity through DF-TTFS encoding. Unless the authors quantify sparsity in raw EEG or pre-TTFS features, the abstract should state more accurately that DA-SNN converts EEG-derived spatial-spectral features into sparse spike-time events for efficient inference.

**中文大意**: 摘要暗示情感 EEG 天然是 event-driven 和 temporally sparse 的，硬件直接利用原始 EEG 稀疏性。但实际上 EEG 先被转换为 PSE feature maps → spatial mapping → LDS smoothing，然后才由 DF-TTFS 施加 spike-time sparsity。摘要应准确表述为：DA-SNN 将 EEG 衍生的 spatial-spectral features 转换为 sparse spike-time events 以进行高效推理。

---

### R4-Q2 — Prose revision

> Several parts of the abstract and main text read overly generic and insufficiently polished. I recommend paragraph-by-paragraph language revision to improve specificity, scientific tone, and technical precision. We note that ChatGPTzero identifies 95% as generated by AI.

**中文大意**: 摘要和正文多处行文过于泛泛、不够精炼，建议逐段润色以提升 specificity、科学语气和技术精确度。另，ChatGPTzero 检出 95% 为 AI 生成。

**中文大意**: Fig. 2 中反复使用 "@" 符号，但图注和正文均未定义其含义。推测可能指 tensor 维度如 C×H×W，需明确说明。

**中文大意**: 文中暗示 C/H/W 是模型输入的 channel/height/width 维度，但 EEG-derived PSE feature map 如何映射为 tensor 并不清楚。EEG channels、PSE segments、spatial grid size 与模型输入维度之间的关系需明确说明。

### R4-Q6 — Equation punctuation

### R4-Q7 — DSGM tensor dimensions

> The channel gate is written as *G*_ch ∈ ℝ^{*C*×1×1}, while the spatial gate is *G*_sp ∈ ℝ^{1×*H*×*W*}. If the pointwise convolution changes the number of channels or the spatial size, then *F*_DS ⊙ *G*_ch ⊙ *G*_sp is not well-defined unless broadcasting, padding, and resizing conventions are explicitly specified.

**中文大意**: channel gate 为 ℝ^{C×1×1}，spatial gate 为 ℝ^{1×H×W}，若 pointwise convolution 改变了通道数或空间尺寸，则 F_DS ⊙ G_ch ⊙ G_sp 在没有明确 broadcasting/padding/resizing 约定的情况下不是良定义的。

### R4-Q8 — B1 model terminology

**中文大意**: 原始论文作者称其为 "B1-model"，建议统一使用此术语以避免歧义，使与参考文献框架的关联更清晰。

### R4-Q9 — Threshold ϑ undefined

> The membrane threshold appears in Eq. (6) and later in the paper, but it is not clearly introduced in the main text before its first use. The authors should define its meaning, units, and role in the B1/ATSNN dynamics.

**中文大意**: membrane threshold ϑ 在 Eq. (6) 及后文中出现，但首次使用前未定义其含义、单位和在 B1/ATSNN 动力学中的角色。

### R4-Q10 — B1 reference discussion insufficient

> A substantial part of the ATSNN formulation appears to rely on the B1 model of Stanojevic et al. However, the paper only briefly cites it. The authors should explain why B1 was selected, especially because the B1 identity mapping is equivalent to a ReLU network, follows similar learning trajectories, and has known gradient-stability properties. The manuscript should also clarify whether the proposed model relies on these properties or only uses B1 as an inspiration.

**中文大意**: ATSNN 的 formulation 很大程度依赖 B1 model，但仅简要引用。应解释为何选择 B1（其 identity mapping 等价于 ReLU 网络、具有相似的 learning trajectories 和已知的 gradient-stability 属性），并阐明 DA-SNN/ATSNN 是依赖这些属性还是仅以 B1 为灵感。

### R4-Q11 — B1 definitions to supplement

> Supplementary Section 2.1 currently gives only a minimal description. It should include the key assumptions of the B1 framework, including the role of the identity mapping, the active/inactive mask, the threshold-crossing slope, and the relation between spike time and ReLU activation.

**中文大意**: 补充材料 Section 2.1 仅有最简描述，应加入 B1 框架的关键假设：identity mapping 的角色、active/inactive mask、threshold-crossing slope、spike time 与 ReLU activation 的关系。

### R4-Q12 — Eq. (6) not strictly closed-form

> Around lines 30–31, the authors state that Eq. (6) is the closed-form solution obtained by integrating Eq. (5). This is not strictly correct. The first expression can be interpreted as the closed-form threshold-crossing time of a simplified B1-type neuron. However, the second expression is not obtained by direct integration of the ODE — it is an additional hybrid censoring or timeout rule imposed at the upper temporal boundary. The authors should reformulate Eq. (6) as a censored first-passage-time model and introduce an explicit active-spike mask.

**中文大意**: Eq. (6) 被声称是 Eq. (5) 积分得到的闭式解，这不严格正确。第一部分可解释为简化 B1 型神经元的 closed-form threshold-crossing time，但第二部分并非 ODE 直接积分结果，而是在上界施加的 hybrid censoring/timeout 规则。应将其重新表述为 censored first-passage-time 模型，并引入显式的 active-spike mask。

---

### R4-Q13 — Eq. (8) numerical safeguard

> The DF-TTFS scaling factor becomes unstable if *V*_max = *V*_min. The authors should use a safeguarded expression such as *S*_p = 2^{⌈log₂(max(*V*_max − *V*_min, *δ*))⌉}, with a small *δ* > 0.

**中文大意**: DF-TTFS 的缩放因子在 V_max = V_min 时会不稳定，应使用带保护的表达式 S_p = 2^{⌈log₂(max(V_max − V_min, δ))⌉}，其中 δ > 0 为一小量。

---

### R4-Q14 — Boundary cases at T_max

> The behavior at *T* = *T*_max is ambiguous. A neuron firing exactly at *T*_max and a silent neuron clipped to *T*_max are represented by the same scalar value. This affects forward propagation, output readout, and gradients. The active-spike mask would resolve this ambiguity.

**中文大意**: T = T_max 时存在歧义——恰好在 T_max 发放的神经元与被 clip 到 T_max 的沉默神经元用同一标量表示，这影响前向传播、输出读取和梯度。引入 active-spike mask 可解决此问题。

---

### R4-Q15 — Gradient-stability argument too weak

> Eq. (9) gives only the local derivative of an active neuron's spike time with respect to a synaptic weight. However, vanishing or exploding gradients in a deep TTFS network are controlled by the product of layer-wise spike-time Jacobians, not by this local temporal offset alone. The B1 paper analyzes the full masked Jacobian, whose spectrum depends on the active-neuron mask and the fixed-slope identity condition. The submitted manuscript does not derive the corresponding Jacobian for DA-SNN and does not show that the adaptive window keeps the multilayer Jacobian spectrum bounded.

**中文大意**: Eq. (9) 仅给出了单个活跃神经元 spike time 对权重的局部导数，但深层 TTFS 网络的梯度消失/爆炸取决于逐层 spike-time Jacobian 的乘积而非单一局部项。B1 论文分析了完整的 masked Jacobian，其谱依赖于 active-neuron mask 和固定斜率 identity 条件。本文未推导 DA-SNN 对应的 Jacobian，也未证明 adaptive window 能使多层 Jacobian 谱保持有界。

---

### R4-Q16 — B1-style Jacobian spectrum experiment

> To support the claim that ATSNN stabilizes gradients, the authors should reproduce an analogue of Fig. 2 from the B1 paper under their own DA-SNN/ATSNN conditions. They should initialize weights using standard deep-learning initialization, compute the layer-wise spike-time Jacobian with active-neuron masks, and examine whether eigenvalues remain inside or near the unit circle. This should be shown for both fixed-window and adaptive-window ATSNN. Without this analysis, the stability claim remains heuristic.

**中文大意**: 为支撑 ATSNN 稳定梯度的声称，应复现 B1 论文 Fig. 2 的类似实验：使用标准 DL 初始化权重，计算带 active-neuron mask 的逐层 spike-time Jacobian，检验特征值是否保持在单位圆内或附近。需同时展示 fixed-window 和 adaptive-window 两种情况。没有这一分析，稳定性声称只是启发式的。

---

### R4-Q17 — Algorithm 1 undefined variable

> In training mode, t̂_{dyn} is assigned only if the valid-spike set *V* is nonempty. If all neurons are silent or censored, then *V* = ∅, line 11 is skipped, and line 13 still uses t̂_{dyn}. The authors should define a fallback rule, such as keeping t̂_{dyn} unchanged or expanding the window when no valid spikes occur.

**中文大意**: 训练模式下 t̂_{dyn} 仅在有效发放集合 V 非空时赋值。若所有神经元沉默或 censored，V = ∅，line 11 被跳过，line 13 仍使用 t̂_{dyn}（未定义）。需定义 fallback 规则，如保持 t̂_{dyn} 不变或无有效发放时扩大窗口。

---

### R4-Q18 — Eq. (11) active-spike mask

> The output logit sums over presynaptic spike times, but if silent neurons are represented numerically by *T*_max, they may be treated as valid late spikes. The logit should include a presynaptic mask so that only neurons that actually fired contribute to the readout.

**中文大意**: Eq. (11) 的输出 logit 对 presynaptic spike times 求和，但如果沉默神经元用 T_max 数值表示，它们可能被当作有效的晚期发放。logit 应包含 presynaptic mask，确保只有真正发放的神经元参与 readout。

### R4-Q19 — INT8 quantization formula

> The signed INT8 quantization uses a factor of 128. Standard symmetric INT8 quantization usually uses 127 to avoid mapping the largest positive value to 128 and then clipping it to 127. The authors should clarify whether the use of 128 is intentional and justify it.

**中文大意**: 有符号 INT8 量化使用因子 128，但标准对称 INT8 量化通常用 127（避免最大正值映射到 128 后被 clip 到 127）。需说明使用 128 是否是故意的并给出理由。

### R4-Q20 — Validation protocol under-specified

> The paper reports high accuracy across SEED, SEED-IV, SEED-V, DEAP, and DREAMER, but it does not clearly state whether splits are subject-dependent, subject-independent, cross-session, cross-trial, or random window-level. This is critical because the preprocessing uses overlapping windows. If adjacent windows from the same trial enter both training and test sets, the reported performance may be inflated.

**中文大意**: 预处理使用了 overlapping windows，但未明确数据划分是 subject-dependent / subject-independent / cross-session / cross-trial 还是 random window-level。若同一 trial 的相邻窗口同时进入训练集和测试集，报告的性能可能是 inflated 的。这是关键问题。

### R4-Q21 — DF-TTFS as hardware efficiency, not accuracy

> In the ablation, replacing DF-TTFS with standard min-max normalization slightly improves accuracy from 96.98% to 97.04%. Therefore, DF-TTFS should not be presented as the best-performing encoding method in terms of accuracy. Its contribution is hardware efficiency through division removal.

**中文大意**: 消融实验中标准 min-max normalization 替换 DF-TTFS 后精度反而略高（96.98% → 97.04%）。因此 DF-TTFS 不应被表述为精度最优的编码方法——其贡献在于通过去除除法操作实现 hardware efficiency。

### R4-Q22 — Robustness experiment insufficient

> Noise is injected into feature maps after fine-tuning, not into raw EEG before preprocessing. This does not model realistic wearable artifacts such as motion, EMG, EOG, electrode detachment, impedance drift, or sweat. The experiment is useful as a synthetic perturbation test, but it is not sufficient evidence for real wearable robustness.

**中文大意**: 噪声注入在 fine-tuned 后的 feature maps 上，而非原始 EEG 预处理之前。这不能建模真实可穿戴伪迹（motion、EMG、EOG、electrode detachment、impedance drift、sweat 等）。该实验作为 synthetic perturbation test 有用，但不足以作为真实可穿戴鲁棒性的证据。

### R4-Q23 — Realistic SNR for robustness

> One reference cites values Relative Noise Level (NL) ranging from 0.05 to 0.125.

**中文大意**: 有文献引用的 Relative Noise Level (NL) 范围在 0.05–0.125，鲁棒性测试应使用此类现实 SNR 水平。
